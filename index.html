Research

ML retrievals
Neural networks that infer temperature, composition and clouds from transmission and emission spectra in seconds instead of hours.
Training data
How choices in building the training set of simulated spectra, such as priors, noise and sampling, bias what the models retrieve.
Degeneracies
Which atmospheric parameters cannot be told apart from a given spectrum, and how to report that uncertainty honestly.
